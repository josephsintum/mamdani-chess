The work is split into one plan per milestone, and each plan is written when its milestone starts. That way it builds on real code, not guesses. Every milestone ends in something that runs and is tested. Plans 01 and 02 are independent and can run in parallel; everything after depends on both.

## Carried forward to later plans

- **The frontend is Svelte 5 (5.57) on SvelteKit 3 (3.0).** Svelte and SvelteKit are separate packages with separate version numbers. SvelteKit 3 changes the setup:
  - There is no `svelte.config.js`; config lives in `vite.config.ts`.
  - `$lib` is gone; use `#lib/…` imports instead.
  - `tsconfig.json` extends `$app/tsconfig`.
  - TypeScript stays on 6.x.
  - Node must be 22.17 or newer.

  Plan 01 sets this up, and every frontend plan must follow it.
- **The honk demo from Plan 01 is throwaway.** Plan 03 deletes it.
- **Rules interpretations made in Plan 02,** listed under "Interpretations made here" in that plan:
  - an "exposes" re-roll ignores the hole,
  - the checkmate re-roll covers any result of the roll,
  - with the Mamdani on the board, a king and one minor piece can still mate.

  If the rules doc rules otherwise, change the pinned test first.
- **House rules are deferred.** There is no `Settings` struct and no House rules panel until test games show a need (live doc, "Watch in test games").
- **Mamdani art is a launch blocker.** The photo on the canvas is a placeholder; Plan 07 must not ship without art we have rights to.

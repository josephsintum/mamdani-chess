Milestone 08 · sketches · 6 Oct 2026

Leaderboards and the player page

The same week of data, drawn three ways, each on a desktop and a phone. A is a weekly list built for a phone, after Duolingo's leagues and Strava's Local Legends. B is a chess.com-style leaderboard: pick a title, see the podium and the list. C opens on your own week and the races you could win, after chess.com's weekly Leagues. The names and numbers are made up. The phones scroll.

A

Weekly list

From Duolingo and StravaChosen

Built for a thumb. All five title tabs fit across a phone. Each title shows who holds it and a chart of where every player stands, after Strava’s Local Legend, with you and the holder marked. Then a Duolingo-style list: a title line under 1st, the top five, and your own row in place among your neighbours, so you see who to pass next.

B

Ranked lists

From chess.com

A plain leaderboard. The titles are a side menu with each holder's name, which becomes a row of chips on a phone. The chosen title gets a podium for the top three, the list below with arrows for who moved since yesterday, and your row pinned at the bottom.

C

Your week first

From chess.com Leagues

Most players will never top a board, so this opens on you. Each title is a race against the person just ahead, scored like a Balatro hand: your number against theirs, and what it takes to pass them. On a phone the races stack, so your best chance is the first thing you see.

P

Player page

From chess.com profiles and Xbox achievement lists

Tap any name on the leaderboard, or your own in the header. It opens on who the player is and how their week is going, then their achievements: milestone ladders with the next tier in view, single-game feats where locked ones show what they take (and secret ones don't), titles they've won, real NYC numbers (what their potholes would cost drivers, their repairs next to the city's), and recent games. History ships in 08a, titles in 08b, achievements in 09.

Still open for milestone 08: which games count (quick match only, or friend games too), and whether a guest who clears cookies starts over.
